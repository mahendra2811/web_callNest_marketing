## Distribution checklist

Before launch:

- Confirm that the Play listing is accessible to the intended audience and the installed version matches the claims.
- Use fictional or redacted customer information in screenshots and recordings.
- Add the Play link to your Instagram profile and verify it opens correctly.
- Prepare the poster, reel and a real screen-recorded demo.

During the campaign:

- Publish the launch poster and LinkedIn caption.
- Post the reel and reuse the same vertical video for Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts where appropriate.
- Share the Status with relevant existing contacts. Follow community rules and only promote where welcome.
- Offer opt-in demonstrations to business owners and ask what was confusing or useful.
- Respond to questions and record recurring feedback. Ask for honest reviews without pressure or incentives.

## Accuracy notes

The Play version captures supported calls after installation and setup; it does not import earlier call history. Incoming capture requires the call-screening role. Outgoing calls may need to be initiated in callNest. Caller names depend on available device information. The app does not record call audio. Do not claim automatic WhatsApp group creation, bulk messaging, guaranteed caller names, or guaranteed capture of every call.
